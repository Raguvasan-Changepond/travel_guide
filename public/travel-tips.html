Chennai Smart Travel Guide

Visa and Entry

At Chennai International Airport, immigration officers are generally welcoming for business travelers.

For business visits, immigration clearance typically takes around 15 to 30 minutes, depending on queue length.

SIM Card and Connectivity

Airtel and Jio offer strong 4G/5G coverage across Chennai and most parts of India.

A practical starter plan is INR 500 (around CHF 5.00) for 50 GB data with 30-day validity.

Wi-Fi Availability

Most hotels provide Wi-Fi access.

Guest Wi-Fi is also enabled at the office for your convenience.

Weather Comfort Tips

  • Use sunscreen during daytime travel.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water.
  • Try local hydration options like coconut water and nungu (ice apple).
  • Prefer light cotton clothes for comfort in Chennai weather.

Transport Guide

Uber and Ola are the easiest options for daily city travel.

Emergency Information

  • Medical emergency number: 108
  • Nearest hospital network: Apollo Hospitals
  • Nearby option: Chettinad Health City, Kamatchi Memorial Hospital

South Indian Cuisine

  • Idli
  • Dosa / Masala Dosa
  • Pongal / Medu Vada
  • Idiyappam with coconut milk
  • Filter coffee
  • Banana leaf meal

Traffic and Construction Note

Chennai is undergoing a major infrastructure upgrade, including metro expansion, flyovers, and road improvement works.

This can add around 10 to 15 minutes to normal commute time in some routes.

Thank you for your patience. The short delay supports long-term faster and smoother city travel.
